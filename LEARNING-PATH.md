# Professional Learning Path

## Level 0 — Baseline

Release, kernel, CPU/RAM, storage, network, SELinux, firewall, services and repositories.

Acceptance: complete Baseline 0.

## Level 1 — Administration

Users/groups, sudo, packages, permissions, processes, logs and systemd.

Acceptance: create and troubleshoot a controlled service.

## Level 2 — Security

SELinux, firewalld, SSH, audit and least privilege.

Acceptance: harden and verify the host.

## Level 3 — Storage and network

LVM, filesystems, mounts, NetworkManager, DNS and diagnostics.

Acceptance: recover from controlled failures.

## Level 4 — Automation

Bash, Python, idempotency, timers and structured logs.

Acceptance: safely automate a recurring task.

## Level 5 — Containers

Podman, rootless execution, images, volumes, networking and systemd integration.

Acceptance: deploy a useful service with documented lifecycle.

## Level 6 — Operations

Monitoring, baselines, incidents, postmortems and runbooks.

Acceptance: diagnose deliberately introduced failures.

## Level 7 — AI-assisted operations

Governed AI, read-only discovery, proposals, controlled execution, evidence and review.

Acceptance: demonstrate AI assistance without silent escalation or unsupported success claims.

## Level 8 — Professional portfolio

Architecture, runbooks, ADRs, evidence, incident reports, Pages and reproducibility.

The goal is operational competence, not command memorization.
