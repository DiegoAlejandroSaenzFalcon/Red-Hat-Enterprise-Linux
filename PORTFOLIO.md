# Professional Portfolio Strategy

## Purpose

Turn the RHEL laboratory into evidence of professional capability without presenting a toy project as production infrastructure.

## What employers should be able to see

### Systems administration

- RHEL lifecycle.
- systemd.
- packages.
- users and privileges.
- troubleshooting.

### Security

- SELinux.
- firewalld.
- SSH.
- audit.
- OpenSCAP.
- least privilege.

### Automation

- Bash.
- Python.
- timers.
- idempotent operations.

### Infrastructure

- storage.
- networking.
- Podman.
- service lifecycle.

### Operations

- baselines.
- monitoring.
- incidents.
- recovery.
- postmortems.

### AI engineering discipline

- explicit AI contracts;
- least privilege;
- human approval;
- evidence;
- model-provider independence;
- separation between suggestion and execution.

## Portfolio evidence hierarchy

A screenshot is useful.

A reproducible lab with documented architecture, commands, verification, evidence and recovery is much stronger.

The public Pages site should therefore emphasize:

**problem → decision → implementation → verification → evidence → lesson**

rather than a list of installed software.
